# LICHOMAT v0.2: polyrhythm and light groove — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build v0.2 of LICHOMAT as accepted in §4 and §7 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-stage2-draft.md`: a tempo per pattern and track (×1/4 … ×4), humanize and track delay, trig conditions in the step's probability byte, track rotation, the per-track settings in an extension block of `.pat` and `tmp.pat`, their UI, a memory budget, the version bump, the owner's checks and release notes, and demo renders.

**Architecture:** The timing core keeps its master grid (96 ticks a step, the master boundaries, the pattern end, the clock outputs) and gives every track a step length L = 96·den/num ticks. Tracks at ×1 stay on the master grid and are generated by the master boundary exactly as in v0.1; a track at another tempo leaves it and steps at boundary items of its own on the transport timeline, with the same one-step lookahead, in its own steps. Delay and humanize add to the hit offset in whole ticks; trig conditions replace the probability test for the codes 101–115. The firmware glue, the menu and the pattern files carry a 4-byte `TrackGroove` per pattern and track.

**Tech Stack:** C11 (firmware: Arm GNU 14.3, `-ffast-math`; host tests: Apple clang 17, UBSan, a strict-FP copy of the core), CMake 3.31, Ninja, ctest, Python 3 (the emulator's `render_test.py`), bash.

## Decisions taken while planning

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Rotation is SHIFT + step 1 (one step earlier) and SHIFT + step 2 (one step later)**, on every page, for the selected voice in the shown pattern. | SHIFT + bar−/bar+ is taken: it sets the track length to the end of the bar (`buttonHandler.c`, `case BUT_PAGE_UP/DOWN`, `if(buttonHandler_getShift())`), and COPY + bar+ copies a bar. SHIFT + steps 1–9 and 11 do nothing in fw 1.7 and v0.1 (`buttonHandler_seqButtonPressed()`: `SEQ_DRUM1`…`SEQ_OP_HH` "single instrument buttons", `SEQ_KIT`, `SEQ_MORPH`, `SEQ_PROJECT`: `break`); the release with SHIFT returns at once (`buttonHandler_seqButtonReleased()`). Steps 1 and 2 are the leftmost pair: left = earlier. |
| D2 | **Delay + humanize time move a hit at most one track step later; the whole offset (swing + shift + delay + humanize) at most 7/8 of a track step earlier.** | The negative limit is the earliest a shift alone reaches (−84 ticks at ×1, −21 at ×4), so the early part is always generated at the boundary before, at least L/8 ticks ahead, at every tempo (also 300 BPM, the core's maximum) and every ratio, and `SEQC_EARLY_HITS` (7) still holds. The positive limit is new (the draft only asked for the negative one): a simulation of the queue (7 tracks at ×4 with ×8 ratchets on every step, shift +7, shuffle 127) gives 24 pending events per track with the limit, 40 with an unlimited +38 ms at 300 BPM, which is 280 > the 256-entry queue. At ×1 the limit never acts (96 ticks ≥ 38 ms up to 300 BPM); at ×4 it acts above about 99 BPM. |
| D3 | **Offsets are whole ticks at the current tempo** (ms × BPM × 384 / 60000, rounded half away from zero): 1.30 ms at 120 BPM. A MIDI Note On leaves at the tick its event fires (`seq_tick()` runs at about 36 kHz, so within about 0.03 ms of it, plus the DIN byte time); the voice starts at the next audio block (≤ 1.45 ms), as in v0.1. | The queue's time unit is the tick; a finer unit would change every queue key. The tick is finer than the audio block at every tempo above 90 BPM. |
| D4 | **At a pattern change the early hits of a track's new first step come from the prediction one master step ahead.** A hit that would lie before the boundary that generates it is not played. | The change is decided one master step ahead (`peekNextPattern()`). For ×1 and faster tracks every early hit is within that step; only tracks at ×3/4 and slower with an offset of more than 96 ticks lose the hit (×3/4: shift −7; ×1/4: shift −2 and beyond), as hits before tick 0 of a start do in v0.1. |
| D5 | **`TrackGroove.tempo` stores the tempo index − 5 (0 = ×1)**, in RAM and in the file; the menu shows and edits the index 0–10 of the draft. | Zero bytes are the defaults everywhere: cleared memory, the backup buffer before its first copy, a block of zeros. | D6 | **The pattern end is the master bar:** the master track (the longest raw length, as before) counted in master steps, whatever its tempo. All tracks restart at their step 1 at a pattern change; without a change they run free. | The draft's choice. A ×1/2 track of 32 steps is the master and ends the pattern every 32 master steps, while its own loop takes 64. |
| D7 | **Swing, shift and ratchets scale with the track step:** swing round(shuffle·64·L/(127·96)) on the track's odd steps (its own step count), shift units of L/8, ratchet hit j at ⌊j·L/k⌋. | D8 | **A track whose tempo is set back to ×1 rejoins the master grid at the next master boundary**; its step until then is shorter. A tempo edit applies from the track's next step boundary. | Keeps every ×1 track on the master grid, where v0.1's code generates it. |

## P1. The per-track settings and the file block

`TrackGroove` (4 bytes, one per pattern and track, `seq_patternSet.groove[64][7]`, 1,792 B in RAM):

| Byte | Field | Range | Menu value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `int8_t tempo` | −5…+5 = index − 5 | index 0–10: ×1/4, ×1/3, ×1/2, ×2/3, ×3/4, ×1, ×4/3, ×3/2, ×2, ×3, ×4 (shown `1/4 1/3 1/2 2/3 3/4  x1 4/3 3/2  x2  x3  x4`) |
| 1 | `uint8_t humTime` | 0–8 ms | 0–8 |
| 2 | `uint8_t humVol` | 0–25 % | 0–25 |
| 3 | `int8_t delay` | −15…+30 ms | 0–45 = ms + 15 |

Step length L of index i: `384 288 192 144 128 96 72 64 48 32 24` ticks. At 120 BPM a step of track at ×3/4 lasts 166.667 ms, ×2/3 187.5, ×1/2 250, ×1/3 375, ×1/4 500, ×4/3 93.75, ×3/2 83.333, ×2 62.5, ×3 41.667, ×4 31.25.

The extension block follows the kit number in `.pat` (after byte 3,662) and in `tmp.pat` (after byte 3,649), 34 bytes:

| Offset | Size | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 4 | `L C H M` (0x4C 0x43 0x48 0x4D) |
| 4 | 1 | version 1 |
| 5 | 1 | tracks: 7 |
| 6 | 28 | `TrackGroove` of tracks 0–6, 4 bytes each, as the table above |

Loading sets the pattern's seven settings to the defaults (all zero) first; then a block with this magic, version 1 and 7 tracks, read completely, replaces them, each field out of range taking its default. A missing, short or unknown block leaves the defaults. fw 1.7 reads up to the kit number and closes the file (F2), so it never sees the block; it loads a v0.2 pattern with every track at ×1, no humanize, no delay, and conditions playing like 100 %, and a pattern it saves has no block.

## P2. The core (seq_core) in v0.2

State added to `seqc_tracks_t` (each track): `bar` (its index on the master grid, as if at ×1), `abs` (its occurrence count; the events' `absStep`), `loop` (loops since the start or a restart), `len` (ticks of its current step), `next` (its next boundary, own grid only); masks `own` (on its own grid), `first` (this master boundary's occurrence starts a run), `restartDone`; `restartAt`/`restartPattern` (a predicted restart); `serial`.

Master boundary at tick n·96 (`seqc_boundary()`), in this order: advance the counters (all `bar`; `index`, `loop`, `abs` of the tracks on the master grid; the wrap of `bar[master]` calls `patternEnd()`; `SEQC_PAT_CHANGED` restarts every track) → master track, shuffle, `stepBoundary(n)` → read the tempo of each grid track (one not at ×1 leaves the grid: `own`, `next = tick + L`) → rolls, late parts, early parts of the grid tracks → merge. A track at ×1 throughout takes exactly v0.1's path and callback order; the new `groove()` reads are pure.

Own-grid boundary (`seqc_trackBoundary()`, item `SEQC_ITEM_TRACK`, after a master boundary at the same tick, tracks in order): advance `index`, `loop`, `abs` → `trackStep(t)` → read the tempo (×1: rejoin the master grid at the next master boundary) → roll, late part, early part of the next step (or of a predicted restart's first step) → merge.

Restart prediction (`seqc_generateEarly()`, when `bar[master]` wraps at n+1 and `peekNextPattern()` says the pattern changes): `restartAt` = (n+1)·96. Grid tracks generate the new first step's early part now; an own-grid track without a boundary of its own before the restart does too, after its stale prediction is removed; one with exactly one such boundary and L ≥ 96 does too, as occurrence `abs + 2`, and that boundary skips it; the others (L < 96) generate it at their last boundary before the restart. `restartAt` is cleared at that master boundary whatever `patternEnd()` returns.

Offsets (V4): `offset = swing (odd occurrence) + shift·L/8 + min(delayTicks + jitter, L)`, then at least −(L − L/8). Hit j at `base + offset + ⌊j·L/k⌋`. Velocity × (100 + volume draw)/100, rounded, at most 127. Draws per occurrence with its main step on: r (probability), then the time (`random() % (2T + 1) − T`, T = humTime in ticks) if humTime > 0, then the volume (`random() % (2V + 1) − V`) if humVol > 0; with both at 0 the draws are v0.1's.

```c
/* LICHOMAT v0.2: the per-track settings of a pattern, stored in the extension block
 * at the end of a pattern file (presetManager.c). Zero bytes are the defaults: x1,
 * no humanize, no delay. */
typedef struct TrackGroove
{
	int8_t		tempo;		// tempo index - 5: -5..+5 = x1/4 x1/3 x1/2 x2/3 x3/4 x1 x4/3 x3/2 x2 x3 x4
	uint8_t		humTime;	// humanize time: random +-0..8 ms
	uint8_t		humVol;		// humanize volume: random +-0..25 %
	int8_t		delay;		// track delay: -15..+30 ms
}TrackGroove;
_Static_assert(sizeof(TrackGroove) == 4, "TrackGroove is stored raw in the extension block (4 bytes)");

#define GROOVE_TEMPO_X1		5	// the menu's tempo index of x1; TrackGroove.tempo is index - 5
#define GROOVE_TEMPO_COUNT	11	// tempo indices 0..10
#define GROOVE_HUM_TIME_MAX	8	// ms
#define GROOVE_HUM_VOL_MAX	25	// percent
#define GROOVE_DELAY_MIN	(-15)	// ms
#define GROOVE_DELAY_MAX	30	// ms

/** g with every field out of range set to its default (a file or a host from elsewhere) */
```

```c
/** boundary step 6: the early parts generated at the master boundary n at tick:
 * - step n+1 of each track at x1 on the master grid, as in v0.1; at a predicted
 *   restart (the master track wraps at n+1 and the pattern changes) the next pattern's
 *   first step;
 * - the next step of each track that leaves the master grid here;
 * - at a predicted restart, the first step of each track on its own grid that has no
 *   boundary of its own before the restart, or one with a step of 96 ticks or more
 *   (that boundary skips it); the others generate it at their last boundary before
 *   the restart (seqc_trackEarly()) */
static void seqc_generateEarly(uint8_t pattern, uint8_t grid, seqc_tick_t tick)

	bool restart = false;

	{

		if (next == SEQC_NO_PATTERN)
			return;			/* the song ends at n+1 */
	}
	if (restart)
	{
		seqc_trk.restartAt = at;
		seqc_trk.restartPattern = next;
		seqc_trk.restartDone = 0;
	}
	for (uint8_t t = 0; t < SEQC_NUM_TRACKS; t++)
	{
		const uint8_t bit = SEQC_BIT(t);
		const uint32_t abs = (uint32_t)seqc_trk.abs[t] + 1u;
		if (!(seqc_trk.own & bit))
		{
			if (restart)
			{
				seqc_generateFirst(t, abs, tick);
				continue;
			}
			const uint8_t step = (uint8_t)seqc_nextIndex(seqc_trk.index[t], seqc_trackLength(next, t));
			if (seqc_skipEarly(t, abs, next, step))
				continue;

			seqc_generate(t, abs, next, step, true, &pl);
		}

		{
			if (seqc_trk.next[t] >= at)
			{

				seqc_generateFirst(t, abs, tick);
			}

				seqc_generateFirst(t, abs + 1u, tick);
		}
		else if (grid & bit)
			seqc_trackEarly(t, pattern, seqc_trk.next[t], tick);	/* it leaves the master grid here */
	}
```

```c
/** boundary steps 1 and 2: advance the counters, and call patternEnd() if the master
 * track wraps on the master grid. Returns false if the song ended there. */
static bool seqc_advanceCounters(seqc_tick_t tick)
{
	seqc_trk.first = 0;
	if (seqc_trk.seeked)
	{
		seqc_trk.seeked = false;	/* the first boundary after a song position: the counters are set */
		return true;
	}

	const uint8_t master = seqc_longestTrack(pattern);
	const bool wrap = seqc_nextIndex(seqc_trk.bar[master], seqc_trackLength(pattern, master)) == 0;

	seqc.absStep++;
	seqc.atStart = false;
	seqc_trk.serial++;
	for (uint8_t t = 0; t < SEQC_NUM_TRACKS; t++)
	{
		const uint8_t len = seqc_trackLength(pattern, t);
		seqc_trk.bar[t] = seqc_nextIndex(seqc_trk.bar[t], len);
		if (seqc_trk.own & SEQC_BIT(t))

		if (seqc_trk.index[t] < 0)
			seqc_trk.first |= SEQC_BIT(t);
		else if (index == 0)

		seqc_trk.index[t] = index;
		seqc_trk.abs[t]++;
	}
	if (seqc_trk.restartAt <= tick)
		seqc_trk.restartAt = SEQC_NO_TICK;	/* the predicted restart is due: over, whatever patternEnd() says */

		return true;

	}
```

```c
static int seqc_msTicks(int ms)
{
	const double bpm = seqc.mode == SEQC_SYNC_INTERNAL ? seqc.masterBpm : seqc_sl.bpm;
	const double t = (double)ms * bpm * ((double)SEQC_TICKS_PER_BEAT / 60000.0);
	return t >= 0.0 ? (int)(t + 0.5) : -(int)(0.5 - t);
}
//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/** the draws of the occurrence in slot d, made once (N3): r for the probability; then,
 * v0.2, the humanize time (ticks, uniform in +-T) if humTime > 0 and the humanize volume
 * (percent, uniform in +-humVol) if humVol > 0 */
static void seqc_draw(seqc_draw_t *d, const TrackGroove *g)
{
	if (d->drawn)
		return;
	d->r = (uint8_t)(1u + seqc.host.random(seqc.host.ctx) % 100u);
	if (g->humTime > 0)
	{
		const int span = seqc_msTicks(g->humTime);
		const uint32_t v = seqc.host.random(seqc.host.ctx);
		d->jitter = (int8_t)(span > 0 ? (int)(v % (uint32_t)(2 * span + 1)) - span : 0);
	}
	if (g->humVol > 0)
		d->velPct = (int8_t)((int)(seqc.host.random(seqc.host.ctx) % (2u * g->humVol + 1u)) - (int)g->humVol);
	d->drawn = true;
}
//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/** the first hit's offset from the grid tick: swing on an odd occurrence, shift, and
 * v0.2's delay + humanize time, at most one track step later; the sum at least 7/8 of a
 * track step earlier (-84 at x1), so the lookahead holds at every tempo */
static int seqc_offset(const seqc_draw_t *d, const TrackGroove *g, StepTiming timing, uint32_t absStep,
		const seqc_place_t *pl)
{
	int groove = seqc_msTicks(g->delay) + d->jitter;
	if (groove > (int)pl->len)
		groove = (int)pl->len;
	const int offset = ((absStep & 1u) ? (int)pl->swing : 0) + seqc_shiftFor(timing.shift, pl->len) + groove;
	const int earliest = -(int)(pl->len - pl->len / 8u);
	return offset < earliest ? earliest : offset;
}
//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/** the velocity of the occurrence's hits: v0.2's humanize volume, rounded, at most 127 */
static uint8_t seqc_humanVel(uint8_t vel, const seqc_draw_t *d)
{
	if (d->velPct == 0)
		return vel;
	const int v = ((int)vel * (100 + d->velPct) + 50) / 100;
	return (uint8_t)(v > 127 ? 127 : v);
}
```

```c
 * another occurrence, or the same one at another (pattern, step), is cleared for it, so a
 * different step gets a new draw. v0.2: a new slot takes the PRE state left by the

static seqc_draw_t *seqc_slot(uint8_t track, uint32_t absStep, uint8_t pattern, uint8_t step, bool first)
{
	seqc_draw_t *d = &seqc_gen.draw[track][absStep & 1u];
	if (!d->valid || d->absStep != absStep || d->pattern != pattern || d->step != step)
	{
		const seqc_draw_t *prev = &seqc_gen.draw[track][(absStep + 1u) & 1u];
		const bool pre = !first && prev->valid && prev->absStep + 1u == absStep && prev->preOut;
		memset(d, 0, sizeof *d);
		d->valid = true;
		d->absStep = absStep;
		d->pattern = pattern;
		d->step = step;
		d->preIn = pre;

 * the trig conditions (SeqStepTypes.h) on the track's loop count, 116..255 like 100. A
 * probability below 100 and the conditions but PRE and !PRE set the PRE state */
static bool seqc_plays(seqc_draw_t *d, uint8_t prob, const seqc_place_t *pl)
{
	static const uint8_t ab[9][2] = { {1,2}, {2,2}, {1,3}, {2,3}, {3,3}, {1,4}, {2,4}, {3,4}, {4,4} };
	bool plays;
```

```c
//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/** LICHOMAT v0.2: move every step of the track in pattern one step later (dir +1) or
 * earlier (-1) within the track length: the step data (volume, probability or condition,
 * note, p-locks), the step timing (flam, shift) and the main-step bit move together, the
 * step at one end wraps to the other, and the steps past the length stay */
void seq_rotateTrack(uint8_t trackNr, uint8_t pattern, int8_t dir)
{
	if((trackNr >= NUM_TRACKS) || (pattern >= NUM_PATTERN) || ((dir != 1) && (dir != -1))) return;
	uint8_t len = seq_patternSet.length[pattern][trackNr];
	if(len == 0) len = 16;
	else if(len > NUM_STEPS) len = NUM_STEPS;
	if(len < 2) return;
	Step *s = seq_patternSet.seq_subStepPattern[pattern][trackNr];
	StepTiming *tm = seq_patternSet.seq_stepTiming[pattern][trackNr];
	const uint8_t wrap = (dir > 0) ? (uint8_t)(len - 1) : 0;
	const Step keep = s[wrap];
	const StepTiming keepTiming = tm[wrap];
	const uint8_t keepOn = seq_intIsMainStepActive(trackNr, wrap, pattern);
	if(dir > 0)
	{
		for(uint8_t i = (uint8_t)(len - 1); i > 0; i--)
		{
			s[i] = s[i-1];
			tm[i] = tm[i-1];
			seq_setMainStep(pattern, trackNr, i, seq_intIsMainStepActive(trackNr, (uint8_t)(i-1), pattern));
		}
		s[0] = keep;
		tm[0] = keepTiming;
		seq_setMainStep(pattern, trackNr, 0, keepOn);
	}
	else
	{
		for(uint8_t i = 0; i + 1 < len; i++)
		{
			s[i] = s[i+1];
			tm[i] = tm[i+1];
			seq_setMainStep(pattern, trackNr, i, seq_intIsMainStepActive(trackNr, (uint8_t)(i+1), pattern));
		}
		s[len-1] = keep;
		tm[len-1] = keepTiming;
		seq_setMainStep(pattern, trackNr, (uint8_t)(len - 1), keepOn);
	}
}
```
