# The groove fixture (plan v0.6 Task 8, spec v0.6 10.2): the onset card's click on Drum 1 alone
# on out 1 (pan left) and on Drum 2 on out 2 (pan left), every other voice on out 2. Kit 0.
# Pattern 0: Drum 1 on all 16 steps with mic values over both nibble signs and the split edges
# (+-5, +-6, +-7, +-78, +-79, +-84), a x2 ratchet with a fine part (step 7), and a shf edit that
# keeps a fine part (step 14: mic +17, then shf +3: +41); Drum 2 at x3/4 on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13
# with fine parts (+5, -6, +17, -19). Pattern 1: Drum 1 16ths with lyb at 100 %, Drum 2 16ths
# with lay at 150 % and dly +3 ms (the shuffle is set by G21_template's script, plan V6-15).
# Pattern 2: Drum 1 on steps 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10: crossings (step 2 at +84 after step 3 at -20;
# step 9's x4 at +40 after step 10 at -40) and a flam that stays (step 5 at +84, step 6).
# Pattern 3: empty, for recording. Patterns 1 and 2 are reached through the pattern page
# (SHIFT + step 16, then the step) and left with mode1 (plan V6-14); a pattern change restores
# the backup buffer, so each pattern is saved before it is left, and autoloads kit 0.
