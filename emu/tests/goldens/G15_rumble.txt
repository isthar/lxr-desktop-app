# G15_rumble: the fx card with the reverb at level 120, tone 20, decay 80, ducked by dr1
# (voice 0) at depth 127 (plan D40, A9). dr1 plays dry on L1/R1 (audio_out1=0), the FX bus
# (dr2, dr3, snare through the slot and the reverb) on L2/R2 (fx_routing=1); at 2.0 s dr2,
# dr3 and snare go to volume 0, so from then on L2/R2 carry the reverb's tail alone, while
# dr1 keeps playing on every beat. At depth 127 a kick's peak (full scale, env >= 0.5) pulls
# the duck gain to 0, so the tail is exactly silent from 3 ms to about 40 ms after each kick
# (2.5026 s and 3.0031 s); without the duck (the broken variant, depth 0) it is not. The
# energy before the kick shows that the tail is there. v0.4 review: the old #expect (the
# tail after the stop) and #broken (rev_level=0) passed with the ducking broken
