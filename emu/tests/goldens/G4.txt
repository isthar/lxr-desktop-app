#! --card song --bpm 120 --seconds 8
#pin panel
#expect led mutes FE F2
#expect lcd pattern Current:   2
#broken-drop down,up,press
# SHIFT + voice 2 and voice 3 mute them (outside the perf page)
1s down shift
+20ms press voice2
+100ms press voice3
+100ms up shift
# a mark starts a new LED window (emu_ledSnapshot()); mutes shows the LEDs lit in 1.32-1.95 s,
# after the mutes, when only the voices that play flash
+100ms mark muted
1.95s mark mutes
# a roll: perf mode (mode 2), step 1 held 500 ms, then back to voice mode (mode 1)
2s press mode2
+100ms press step1 500
+600ms press mode1
# pattern 1: SHIFT + step 16 opens the pattern page, then step 2
3s down shift
+20ms press step16
+80ms up shift
+100ms press step2
4s knob 1 +30
# the pattern change waits for the bar's end at 4 s
4.5s mark pattern
5s fader 1 0.3
7.5s mark end
