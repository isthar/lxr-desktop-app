#! --card init --bpm ext --no-start --seconds 7 --param prescaler_clock_in=1
#expect energy L1 -30 0.6s..2.9s
#expect energy L1 -30 5.1s..6.4s
#expect midi DIN 9n 0..0 3s..5s
#broken --bpm 120
0ms reset on
10ms jack-clock 120 24
# the gate opens: the sequencer runs from step 1
500ms reset off
# closed: no step plays until 5 s. After a close at 3 s the init kit's tails last until
# 9.04 s (L1 peak 42 in 4s..5s), so the closed gate is checked by its Note Ons
3s reset on
5s reset off
6.5s reset on
