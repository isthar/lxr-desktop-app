# out 0 St1 ... 6 FX. Notes: coarse 31 is G1 (49 Hz), +12 an octave; fine 64 is in

# (build/wt-v07), the one the sound lab used.
#
# Each voice is PROSTO's (kits/prosto.txt) or a recipe's edits in the recipe's order.
#
# Levels. The kit keeps PROSTO's kick-to-hat balance (the kick at vol1 86 against PROSTO's
# 100, the hat at vol6 127 as in PROSTO) and B6's balance of everything else to the kick.
# The sound lab had turned its kick down (the fader 0.35, gain = fader^2) only so that the
# rest could be heard; the ghost kick (the fader 0.4), the boom and the riser (the faders
# 0.146 and 0.229) are set against that kick. A fader and vol are the same gain (after the
# drums' drive, before the Cymbal's distortion), so each vol is the recipe's vol x its
# fader^2 / 0.1225 x 0.86: the kick's step from the fader 0.35 to vol1 86, the highest kick
# at which the ghost kick keeps its balance with vol2 127. Measured with all six faders at
# 1: B6's voices within 0.3 dB of the recipe relative to the kick, the hat 17 dB further
# under it than in B6, the peak on out 1 -5.1 dBFS (-4.4 in the demo below, with the knock).
# The reverb's duck follows the kick's peak (the depth x twice the peak, at most all), so
# the louder kick would duck the reverb 7 times deeper than in B6: rev_duck_depth 18
# (127 x 12.25 / 86) keeps B6's duck; with 127 the reverb would duck all the way under
# each kick.
#
# No freeze: B2, B4 and B6 have none.
#
# The demo plays the 'ghost' pattern (tools/kit_build.py GHOST_PATTERN) at 143 BPM,
# straight: the kick, the ghost kicks, the knock on 2 and 4 and the hat layer, without the
# riser and the boom. Both are rare: the generator's rar places them (the riser sty 1, bars
# 8 and 16; the boom sty 2, bars 1 and 9).
