# Each voice is PROSTO's (kits/prosto.txt) or a recipe's edits in the recipe's order.
#
# Levels. The sound lab set the balance partly with the faders (gain = fader^2), which a
# kit does not keep: the kick at 0.35, the ghost kick at 0.4, the roar at 0.09. A fader
# and vol are the same gain (after the drums' drive, before the Cymbal's distortion), so
# each vol here is the recipe's vol x its fader^2, measured: with all six faders at 1 the
# kit plays the recipe's balance, and the kick still ducks the reverb as in the recipe
# (the duck follows the kick's level). The hat is already at its maximum (vol6 127, fader
# 1), so the kick comes down to it (vol1 12) instead of the rest going up: the kit plays
# at the recipes' level, a peak of -10 to -13 dBFS in the demos. Turn the output up.
#
# Freeze. B7 freezes the reverb's tail for bars 2-3 and 10-11 of its 16, an event of the
# render: a kit always saves freeze off and a step cannot p-lock it. On the LXR freeze is
# turned by hand: SHIFT + step 3 in the performance mode (or frz on the reverb's
# sub-page) on the bar after the roar, and off again two bars later.
#
# The demo plays the 'ghost' pattern (tools/kit_build.py GHOST_PATTERN) at 143 BPM,
# straight: the kick, the ghost kicks, the tick on 2 and 4 and the hat layer, without the
# figure and the roar. The roar is rare: the generator's rar places it (sty 2: bars 1 and 9).
