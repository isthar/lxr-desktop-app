# Each voice is PROSTO's (kits/prosto.txt) or a recipe's edits in the recipe's order.
#
# Levels. The kit keeps A9's balance of the bed to the kick and brings the kick as close to
# PROSTO's kick-to-hat balance as the bed allows. The sound lab had turned its kick down
# (the fader 0.35, gain = fader^2) only so that the rest could be heard; the bed (the fader
# 0.6) is set against that kick. A fader and vol are the same gain (after the drums' drive,
# before the Cymbal's distortion), so vol1 and vol2 are A9's vol x its fader^2 / 0.1225 x
# 0.35. A9's bed note needs 3.6 times the kick's vol (123 at 0.6 against 100 at 0.35), so
# with vol2 127 the kick is vol1 35 (PROSTO's 100). The hat and the snare, which PROSTO
# balances against the kick, come down with it: vol6 52 (127 x 35 / 86), so the hat sits as
# far under the kick as in ECHO (42.1 dB in RMS against ECHO's 42.0, both dry, measured),
# and vol4 51 (C2's 124 x 52 / 127). Measured with all six faders at 1: A9's voices within
# 0.1 dB of the recipe relative to the kick, the bed 8.2 dB under the kick as in A9, the hat
# 16.7 dB further under it than in A9, the peak on out 1 -6.6 dBFS (-5.5 with the events,
# -6.2 in the demo below).
# The reverb's duck follows the kick's peak (the depth x twice the peak, at most all), so
# the louder kick would duck the reverb about 3 times deeper than in A9: rev_duck_depth 44
# (127 x 12.25 / 35) keeps A9's duck and with it the rumble's shape. The downlifter and the
# stutter play in A9's reverb, not in their own (B3's and B5's predelay, decay and tone
# would move the bed), so their vol is measured here again: each about 17 dB under this
# kit's groove, as in set B.
#
# Freeze. B5 freezes the reverb from step 13 of bar 8 to the middle of bar 9 (and of 16 to
# 1), an event of the render: a kit always saves freeze off and a step cannot p-lock it. On
# the LXR freeze is turned by hand: SHIFT + step 3 in the performance mode (or frz on the
# reverb's sub-page) on the last beat of the stutter's bar, and off again two beats into
# the next. The frozen reverb holds the rumble too: for those three beats it takes no new
# kick (a frozen reverb takes no input).
#
# The demo plays the 'bed' pattern (tools/kit_build.py BED_PATTERN) at 143 BPM, straight:
# the kick, the bed, the tick on 2 and 4 and the hat layer, without the downlifter and the
# stutter. Both are rare: the generator's rar places them (the downlifter sty 2, bars 1 and
# 9; the stutter sty 1 den 4, bars 8 and 16).
