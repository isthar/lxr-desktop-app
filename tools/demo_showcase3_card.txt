# The card of the third showcase track (tools/demo_showcase.sh --track 3), from the owner's pattern
# of 2026-11-04 in the BCCO / Mutual Rytm style: the PULS kit (slot 56 of the kits branch) as kit 0,
# rebuilt voice by voice, the one pattern the track plays, and eight mute scenes. Run on a seeded card
# whose 00-*.SND is PULS, with --keep-card; the track's render then plays the kept card. Values as
# the menu stores them (emu/render/render_params.h); step_prob 101-115 are the trig conditions 1:2
# 2:2 1:3 2:3 3:3 1:4 2:4 3:4 4:4 Fil !Fl 1st !1s Pre !Pr; track_tpl 0-7 are off swg lay lyb rlx 2bt
# psh drg; step_note is 60 for the voice's own pitch, +1 a semitone; slide_time is x 4 ms.
#
# The owner's pattern (X loud, x a ghost, | the end of a loop):
#   kick with rumble   X...X...X...X...
#   ghost kick         ...x..X....x..X.
#   shaker             xxX|              (a 3-step loop against the bar)
#   open hat           ..X...X...X...X.
#   snare              ....X.......X...
#   industrial perc    A on 1 of bar 1, b on 12 of bar 2: a call and its answer, long tail
#   bass (Drum 3)      ..X....X..x|      (an 11-step loop: odd, not irritating)
